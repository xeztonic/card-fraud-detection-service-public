Dashboard Metrics Explained

  • Total Transactions: total number of analyzed transactions stored in the database.
  • Average Risk Score: average fraud risk score across all stored transactions. Range: 0 to 100.
  • Alerts Sent: number of transactions for which an admin or customer alert was sent.
  • High Risk Rate: percentage of transactions classified as High Risk.
  • Risk Category Distribution: visual count of Legitimate, Suspicious, and High Risk transactions.
  • Live Transaction Monitoring: simulates a stream of incoming transactions and stores each analyzed transaction in the database.

Risk score ranges:

  • Legitimate: below 30
  • Suspicious: 30 to 69.99
  • High Risk: 70 or above

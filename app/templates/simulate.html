Transaction Simulation Fields

  • Transaction Amount: transaction value. In the IEEE-CIS dataset, the currency is anonymized, so it is treated as a generic monetary amount.
  • Transaction Time: relative transaction timestamp from the dataset, not an actual date.
  • Card Identifier: anonymized card-related feature, not the real card number.
  • Card Secondary Identifier: additional anonymized card feature.
  • Card Type: card network/type, for example visa, mastercard, discover.
  • Address Region: anonymized address/region feature.
  • Transaction Email Domain: email domain used in the transaction, for example gmail.com.
  • Distance: distance-related behavioral feature. Higher values may indicate unusual transaction behavior.
  • Product Code: anonymized transaction product category. Possible values include W, C, H, R, and S. Their exact meaning is not publicly disclosed in the dataset.
  • Customer Email: email address used only for sending the security notification to the cardholder.

Risk Scoring Explained

  • Base ML Probability: raw probability estimated by the trained machine learning model.
  • Behavioral Risk Adjustment: additional risk added based on transaction amount, distance, email domain, and product type.
  • Final Risk Score: final score from 0 to 100, calculated from the base ML probability plus behavioral adjustments.
  • Risk Category: final classification based on the Final Risk Score.

Risk score ranges:

  • Legitimate: below 30
  • Suspicious: 30 to 69.99
  • High Risk: 70 or above
